Patch 1.2.0.23
- Added popup notification warning that FTW is unsupported when entering world on FTW
- Added popup notifications warning that Calamity Overhaul and Infernum are incompatible when entering a world with respective mod
- Removed Abandoned Remote
- Fixed Acidwood Sword and Driftwood Sword not receiving a reworked broadsword swing animation
- Fixed various Mutant projectiles drawing incorrectly

- Recipe changes:
- - All recipes that previously required multiple consumable rogue weapons now only use 1 copy of each weapon due to them becoming non-consumable.
- - Added Banner Recipe for Antlion Skewer.
- -  Desert Prowler Enchantment now uses Antlion Skewer and Nasty Cholla instead of Storm Spear and Stormjaw Staff.
- - Sulphurous Enchantment now uses Any Sulphurous Sea Fruit instead of Rusty Beacon Prototype.
- - Titan Heart Enchantment now uses Monolith Hammer and Any Astral Fruit instead of Stress Pills and Ursa Sergeant.
- - Daedalus Enchantment now uses Darkecho Greatbow instead of 20 Frozen Candles.
- - Outlaw's Essence now uses Spore Knife instead of Feather Knife, and no longer uses Sludge Spotch.

- Removed the following balance changes to items:
- - Soma Prime: Increased damage by 20%
- - Svantechnical: Increased damage by 10%
- - Rainbow Party Cannon: Decreased damage by 30%
- - Universe Splitter: Decreased damage by 50%.
